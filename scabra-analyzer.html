Qualified Rehabilitation Expenses — Key Rules

✓ Generally Eligible
  • Structural renovations
  • Mechanical, electrical, plumbing (MEP)
  • HVAC systems
  • Interior buildout tied to adaptive reuse
  • Facade restoration
  • Environmental remediation (if required)
  • Architectural & engineering fees (if after NOI)
✗ Generally Ineligible
  • Land acquisition costs
  • New construction (non-rehabilitation)
  • Furnishings and equipment
  • Financing costs and loan fees
  • Developer fees (in most cases)
  • Soft costs exceeding statutory limits
  • Costs incurred before NOI filing
⚠ Timing Risk — Pre-NOI Costs
  • A&E fees incurred before NOI: at risk under current statute
  • HB 5252 ties deadline to building permit — clearer safe harbor
  • Costs incurred after NOI filing remain eligible even if permit deadline missed under HB 5252
  • Both regimes require accrual-basis accounting for the 80% threshold test

Capital Stack Risk Analysis

Analyze the financing impact of HB 5252's proposed prohibition on pledging SCABRA credits as debt collateral.

🚨
Critical Regulatory Conflict: RR #26-1 vs. HB 5252 § 12-67-140(B)(5)(c)

Revenue Ruling #26-1 expressly permits pledging future SCABRA credits as loan collateral. HB 5252 would expressly prohibit any pledge, assignment, hypothecation, or collateralization of credits as security for debt. Until the bill is enacted, RR #26-1 governs — but lenders and developers must plan for both scenarios now.

Alternative Capital Structures Compatible with HB 5252

Convert mezzanine debt to preferred equity with priority distributions tied to credit realization. No collateral pledge needed; investor protected via equity waterfall.

Recommended
💼

Credit Purchase Agreement

Pre-negotiate a binding purchase agreement with a state tax-credit investor at a set price per dollar of credit, providing exit certainty without a collateral assignment.

Common Practice
🏦

Increased Equity Cushion

Require higher developer equity contributions upfront (30–40% vs. 20%) to reduce lender risk previously mitigated by the credit pledge.

Conservative
📋

Completion Guaranty / Payment Bond

Enhance lender protection via personal or corporate completion guaranties or payment bonds rather than a credit collateral assignment or hypothecation.

Bottom Line for Practitioners

✅ HB 5252 Makes It Easier to Qualify

  • "Unoccupied" standard vs. "income-producing" requirement
  • Churches, public schools, non-profits, and government buildings now eligible
  • Building permit milestone replaces "first expense" deadline
  • Simplified state-owned property process (separate tier abolished)

🚫 HB 5252 Makes It Harder to Finance

  • Total prohibition on credit collateralization
  • Bridge and mezzanine lenders lose perfected security interest in credits
  • Existing capital stacks must be restructured
  • May significantly tighten credit availability for adaptive reuse
